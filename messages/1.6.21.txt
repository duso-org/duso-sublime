Duso 1.6.21 Support
===================

Updated Sublime Text plugin for Duso 1.6.21

WHAT'S NEW:
- Added syntax highlighting for new builtin functions
- Support for: decrypt, encrypt, fibonacci, hmac, send_websocket, toregex,
  verify_ed25519, websocket

REQUIREMENTS:
- Duso 1.6.21 or later must be installed
- LSP package for Sublime Text

For Duso release notes, visit: https://duso.rocks
